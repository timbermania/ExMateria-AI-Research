# LFO Subslot Residue

LFO modulation state that FFT holds in main-RAM chan-blocks (not SPU registers) and that can survive savestates: when a savestate is captured mid-effect, active effect-pool slots carry non-zero LFO subslot accumulators that were armed by D7/D8/D9-family opcodes *before* the capture window, so Godot cannot reconstruct them from SMD bytecode alone — and SPU-side snapshots (`spu_initial_state.json`) cannot capture them in principle. On `haste_no_music` (2026-05-23) all four active slots (2–5, voices 18–21) carried such residue; the 2026-05-23 status update read those subslots as dormant (`active_dir=0x0000`) and displaced the LFO-residue root cause onto the +48-cad timeline-driver lag (see [[Effect Sound Timing]]) — but the 2026-05-24 `VOICE_21_FAITHFUL_LFO_RESIDUE_REPLAY` doc supersedes that reading: with the lag fixed and KEYON/KEYOFF paired, the remaining voice 21 cos_dist 0.427 traces to voice 20's still-running subslot-2 mid-cycle pitch LFO resumed by PCSX from main RAM, and smd-player now mode-dispatches all four subslots and folds the subslot-2 mode-0/2 bias into `fft_effective_voice_sinc`. smd-player now primes Godot's `lfo_blocks` from the `chan_lfo_residue.json` snapshot via `set_voice_lfo_subslot` (gated on the `active_dir` bit 0), and the paired `probe_lfo_subslot{0,1,2,3}_state` probes track subslot state per cadence. The 2026-05-18 ICE V21 doc (E024, `ice_no_music`) adds the init asymmetry: FFT's `play_sound` per-channel init clears all four sub-slots' `active_dir` on audible primaries (PC `0x80013D5C`) while preserving every other sub-slot field, silent-driver binds skip that init and retain savestate active state, and smd-player's `_apply_lfo_state_seed` now mirrors the asymmetry by seeding `lfo_sub_active`/`lfo_sub_dir_flags` only for `is_silent_driver` channels (iter-35 unified sub-slot 0 onto the `lfo_sub_*[0]` arrays and closed the residual V21 seed deficit).
