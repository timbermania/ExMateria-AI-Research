# Cure 4 Audio Parity

State of knowledge for the `cure_4` effect-sound session (the for-each-spawn variant of the cure timeline), as of the 2026-05-13 bit-0x1000-gate experiment. Pair-slot allocation is time-aligned between Godot and PCSX: slot 4 holds pair 0 for the whole trace and slot 2 holds pair 1 then pair 2 after reuse, so the previously claimed "Godot allocates 3 pair slots vs PCSX's 2" divergence was an artifact of counting raw pool occupancy across all time (both sides allocate three times; the 2026-05-14 autoload-plan validation showed PCSX's first two are in-capture play_sound calls at real-time cad=96 that precede the FIRST_OPCODE_FIRED anchor, not pre-trace residue). Known residual divergences at that date: voice 18 silent (the silent driver of pair 1's audible never KONs because `chan_92_value` stays 0 in Godot) and a 24-cadence drift on the first keyframe fire (PCSX cadence 96 vs Godot 72; the 2026-05-13 bisection traces it to PCSX's pre-trace sound-track ticker firing — see [[Effect Sound Timing]]). 2026-05-15 update: after the noise-LFO PRNG gate fix, the cure_4_no_music wf_idx=6/7 noise LFOs now match PCSX bit-exact — voice 18/19 pitch_bend value sets are element-for-element identical and the cadence-497 PRNG desync is resolved (see [[Noise LFO PRNG]]). The 2026-05-13 CAUSE_A_PRESTAGE_TIMING doc confirms voice 18's first KEYON sees `walker_flag_word=0x095` (no 0xAC before its first Note), with its first 0x1FF arm only at cad=242. 2026-05-14 resolution: voice 18's wrong-instrument prestage is root-caused and fixed — FFT's slot allocator seeds new slots with `instrument_idx = 0` (resolving to WAVESET inst[1] via the +1 loader rule), and Godot's `_prestage_first_instrument` now mirrors that `allocator_default` when the first Note precedes the first AC, so voice 18's first KEYON is bit-exact on `start_addr`/`loop_addr`/`adsr2`; because voice 18 is a noise voice (its sample registers don't gate the mix), the residual audio divergence now lives in the pitch baseline, not the sample (see [[SPU Voice Engine]]). The 2026-05-14 pitch-default follow-up resolved that pitch-baseline residual: FFT's slot-bind path actively writes the default pitch register (`note_to_pitch(60, fine_tune)` = 4078 on voice 18, not savestate residue), and Godot's `_bind_slot` now seeds `channel.bmidi_baseline_byte = 60` for every slot, so voice 18's first KEYON `raw_pitch` is 4078 — bit-exact vs PCSX (was 128) — leaving the ~119 ms onset shift and the `adsr1` high-byte as the remaining voice-18 contributors. The 2026-05-14 KEYON_ADSR1_MASK_AND_ORDERING_PATCH doc root-caused that `adsr1` high-byte residual to the `& 0x80FF` mask in `_key_on_voice` — a misreading of FFT's three ADSR1 RMW helpers, which rewrite their slices and never clear the attack-rate field (PCSX first-KEYON `adsr1` carries a non-zero attack byte on all four `cure_4` effect voices: 0x32FF/0x35FF/0x34FF/0x2DFF vs Godot's 0x00FF); the mask has since been removed. The 2026-05-14 autoload-plan validation (§6 of `CURE_4_EFFECT_BIN_AUTOLOAD_PLAN`) adds the savestate-side picture: `DAT_80032A54` holds `0x9101` for the whole cure_4_no_music capture window (bit 0x1000 set, all three play_sound calls pass the 0x800125C0 gate), the effect-sound chan pool at 0x80037198 is captured mid-cast (walker_flag_word=0x54 pre-armed on slots 2/3/6/7, note_duration=45 on slots 4/5, all chan_word_0=0), and the engine constructs chan_word_0=0x0409 over the first ~98 IRQs — so the remaining divergence is the trace-anchor reference frame, not the slot mechanism. The 2026-05-14 startup-onset investigation quantified that residual: all four `cure_4_no_music` voices first KEYON 5271 samples (119.5 ms) earlier on Godot than PCSX — a uniform cluster shift, not per-voice drift — because PCSX spends 98 metronomic RCnt2 pulses (first `play_sound` at raw_pulse 97, anchor at raw_pulse 99) between savestate-load and first opcode while Godot's session starts the mixer cold and dispatches at tick 0; no SMD opcodes dispatch pre-anchor on PCSX, and the slot-struct residue at `play_sound` entry is confined to non-gating fields (baseline byte 0x003C, mirrored by the `_bind_slot` seed). The orchestrator-side partial fix shipped in the same doc — capture starts at first opcode dispatch via `START_CAPTURE_PENDING` instead of savestate-load — dropped voice 19's `cos_dist` from 0.226 to 0.124 with probe row counts unchanged. 2026-05-19 update: the post-Step-C voice_18 residual (cos_dist 0.133/0.141) was root-caused to mix-loop transposition — PCSX reads the global LFSR contiguously {1..45} per 45-sample batch while the pre-refactor Godot mixer read it strided {1,5,…,177}; both sides advance the LFSR 180× per batch, so trajectories align at batch boundaries and the in-batch read order was the entire residual (voice 18 is cure_4's only noise voice; its ADSR + vol_l/r are parity-verified, so the divergence reduces to the LFSR stream). The batched outer-voice/inner-sample mirror has since landed in the native core (commit 1a467f586, `fft_render_mix_batch`). 2026-05-20 update: the post-batched-mixer voice_18 residual (cos_dist 0.035–0.041, the audible "spread out static") was root-caused to the per-voice WAV extraction path — `render_voice_pcm16` drove the per-output-sample `fft_render_mix_frame`, giving v18 stride-4 LFSR reads instead of the stride-1-within-45 of the PCSX/batched path (13–14× HF spectral tilt, lag-1 +0.23 vs +0.75) — and fixed by routing the extractor through `render_mix_batch` in `kMaxBatch = 45`-sample steps; post-fix voice_18 sits at 0.0056/0.0066 (<0.01 target met) with the `cure_no_music` regression clean. The 2026-05-19 savestate-residue doc adds the pre-anchor SPU picture: voice 18 is a savestate-residue noise voice (on from capture sample 0, residue KOFF at 68977, cure_4 re-KEYON at 70470), every KON/KOFF event pairs on `cadence_index` exactly, so the residual lives in absolute-sample alignment (voice-18 onsets ~20–22k samples later on Godot; the noise-LFSR state at each KEYON) — addressed by residue-voice seeding at session start (see [[Savestate Residue Voice]]). The 2026-05-19 walker-miss investigation (authored against the 0.127/0.153 post-batched-mixer voice_18 baseline, before residue seeding) root-causes that dominant residual to the third play_sound's walker arm: PCSX's post-anchor play_sound at cad 494 arms `walker_flag_word = 0x1FF` for voices 18+19 in `Hyp_instrument_data_loader` (drained by the cad-495 walker as a full re-init), while Godot's static `ac_before_first_note` deferral parked it in `walker_seed_pending` until the cad-498 note KON — collapsing two PCSX fan-outs into one; the post-anchor immediate arm (gate `_Trace._post_anchor and (channel_word_0 & 0xC) != 0`, commit 417561ef6) now mirrors the ROM while the pre-anchor deferral is preserved for the residue-capture interaction (see [[Effect Sound Timing]]). The 2026-05-13 KON_CLUSTER_AND_CATCHUP_DRIFT doc decomposes the post-f8a6e691 +3 walker-fanout cluster into two root causes — Godot's synthetic `first_keyon_committed` 0x1FF full-arm (one extra fire per cluster probe; since removed in favour of the `walker_seed_pending` deferred arm) and one extra tail-end entity-catchup `cadence_body` iteration (Godot cadences 495/496/497 vs PCSX 495/498, root cause open at the doc date).
