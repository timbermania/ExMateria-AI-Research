# Effect Sound Timing

Effect sound playback in E###.BIN is scheduled by frame-based sound tracks in the timeline section, not by the SMD data itself: opcodes 40/41 tick the tracks each frame with a per-keyframe duration counter (`time_values[N]` = exact frames between sound N and sound N+1, the sound fires the instant its keyframe is entered), each keyframe's `sound_id` (0/1 = none, ≥2 = config index − 2) is resolved through the effect-flags sound-channel configs (five selection modes in `lookup_sound_effect`), and the resolved sound triggers an even/odd channel pair into the feds/SMD engine, at NTSC 30 FPS (1 frame = 33.33 ms). The 2026-04-16 working document reads the sound section as repeating uint16 (frame, sound ID) trigger pairs, an alternative model to the opcode-driven timing above, recorded as a low-evidence point. A 2026-05-13 probe bisection adds pre-trace behaviour: on PCSX the parent sound-track ticker runs 11–12 effect-ticks (and binds 2 FEDS channel pairs) before the SMD interpreter's first opcode dispatch, a pre-tick Godot's tick-0 start does not reproduce, driving the 24-cadence first-keyframe drift (see [[Cure 4 Audio Parity]]). The 2026-05-13 CAUSE_A_PRESTAGE_TIMING doc pins the cadence-anchor asymmetry: PCSX's `FIRST_OPCODE_FIRED` flips mid-IRQ in the first opcode dispatch, while Godot's `_cadence_anchored` latches one IRQ later, so Godot's post-AC cad=0 walker pass is pre-anchor (probe row dropped) and PCSX's cad=1 cluster visibility comes from the post-gate arm. The 2026-05-19 walker-miss doc adds the residue side of that pre-anchor deferral: pre-anchor walker drains land in `spu_initial_state.json` residue rather than the capture WAV, so Godot's first play_sound keeps its 0x1FF arm deferred (`walker_seed_pending`) to the first post-anchor KON and must not re-fire the pre-anchor arms. The 2026-05-24 HASTE_VOICE_21 doc pins the live runtime cursor of that per-frame ticker on the effect entity itself (+0x34 kf_idx / +0x36 frames_left, four IRQ-gated writer PCs, ~75 Hz effective tick), lands a defensive `--animation-vm-snapshot` consumer that seeds Godot's channel cursors from a PCSX snapshot (verified on haste's voices 18/19 and full_mix; still a no-op on haste because every captured cursor is out of range), and measures the +2 kf-index offset between PCSX's keyframe table and Godot's stripped `sound_tracks.json`. The 2026-05-24 KEYON-LAG investigation (post-seed-fix) pins the residual's mechanism: Godot's clean-start kf-0 walk vs PCSX's pre-advanced entity cursor (kf 2 / 19 frames left at FIRST_OPCODE_FIRED) is the ~48-cad late-KEYON origin, the sign is session-dependent (zombie early / haste late), and the `--timeline-frame-anchor` `maxi(0, N)` skip only corrects the early direction. The 2026-05-17 ice (E024) doc adds the multi-trigger case: 5 sound-trigger keyframes across 3 animate_tick channels, 4 firing post-anchor (cad 101/101/937/1069) versus cure's single-trigger control. The 2026-05-19 ICE V16 first-note doc (post-`ac89b462` regression sweep) isolates a deterministic +2-cadence drift on V16's first audible burst (PCSX cad 103 vs Godot 105) that the dispatcher converges by cad 135, re-drifts +3 at the `0x90` EndBar (253/256), and proves independent of the LFO sub-slot seeder gates (V16's savestate sub-slots `active_dir=0`). The 2026-05-21 ICE V16 play_sound trigger-granularity doc (follow-up to the first-note deficit) roots that +2-cad drift in architecture rather than state: FFT's animate_tick sound-track walker decrements the keyframe duration at RCnt2-IRQ (sub-tick) granularity and fires kf=3 mid-outer (post-anchor cad 101), while Godot's SoundTrackController decrements each channel's frames_left once per outer-tick and can only fire at sub-tick-0 boundaries (outer 34, cadence-labelled 103) — a deterministic 2-sub-tick (~8.3 ms) wall-clock delay that the lag-aligned scoring does not collapse (raw 0.227 vs 0.233 at lag −153), with the kf_idx/track_idx state machines pairing exactly on both sides at every fire. The 2026-05-21 ICE V16 STC IRQ-granular refactor doc calibrates that per-effect sub-tick offset directly from PCSX `cadence_index % 8` — ice_no_music fires at sub-tick 5 (post-anchor cad 101), cure_4_no_music at sub-tick 6 (cad 494), ruling out the global-constant fix; the seven-effect sweep shows only these two sessions have post-anchor animate_tick `play_sound` calls at all — and lands the FFT-faithful Option C fix (the STC's once-per-outer keyframe decrement gated on `sub_tick == fire_sub_tick` from a per-effect `stc_fire_sub_tick` timeline-header field, called per sub-tick from the render loop and from godot-learning's `SoundSubsystem` adapter per ADR 0011/0012), validating ice `voice_16.cos_dist` median 0.0273 → 0.0156 (5 runs) and cure_4 `voice_18` 0.006/0.007 (2 runs) with no regression, and correcting the ice drift's wall-clock reading to 3 sub-ticks (3 IRQs) with the +2 a cadence-label consequence of the `tick_irq_start` bump-ordering. The 2026-05-17 formula-stage-gate doc confirms the downstream-probe side of that gating: PCSX's pitch/vol formula-stage probes gate on `FIRST_OPCODE_FIRED` alone (the first event dispatch fires before the first FUN_80017118 walker pass, so cad=0 stage rows emit on PCSX), making Godot's `_post_anchor` gate on the two formula-stage helpers one tick too strict (4 cad=0 stage-groups, 20 pitch + 16 vol rows, suppressed on reraise_no_music) — the gate has since been removed and both probes pair exactly. The 2026-05-18 TIMELINE_DRIVER_ANCHOR_LAG consolidation doc pins the sound-track tick structure — FUN_801A51BC calls `advance_p1_sound_track` (FUN_801A478C) ×3 per frame tick for the phase-1 tracks, plus a conditional ×3 for the phase-2 tracks once frame ≥ phase2_start — and the two entry paths to 478C (post-anchor via 51BC; pre-anchor via a local jump at PC 0x801A4770 inside the per-unit handler FUN_801A4530, the savestate-restore initial-state replay), with the −23/−24-cad zombie lag landing only on the silent drivers (v16–19 bind solely via play_sound, no pre-KOFF clear) while the audible primaries v20–21 re-anchor via the bytecode KON_ARM and pair perfectly (zombie pre-suppression pair_rate 0.2632).
