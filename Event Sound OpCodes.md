# Event Sound OpCodes

Event instructions `{21}` Sound Effect and `{6B}` Background Sound play from FFT's two **global** SFX banks — not per-effect `E###.BIN` audio: the **system** bank (`SOUND/SYSTEM.SED` → `system.feds`, category 0x0000, 167 sounds) for one-shot UI/combat/cinematic cues, and the **env** bank (`SOUND/ENV.SED` → `env.feds`, category 0x0001, 23 of 27 IDs in use) for ambient, mostly-looping weather/environment sound. The `{6B}` semantics are now fully reverse-engineered and live-verified (2026-07-01): it is an asynchronous fire-and-forget opcode that spawns a cooperative task (kind `0x35`) playing an env-bank sound under handle `0x10000 | Sound` and linearly ramping its volume from the byte-1 **start volume** to the target over `Time` frames — the wiki's "Echo" byte is the ramp's *start volume* (the opcode has no echo parameter), and Stacking selects tracked (replaceable) vs. overlay playback; `{6A}` Edit BG Sound re-runs the same ramp on the playing sound. Godot implements both (2026-07-02) with a fading, looping, replaceable bg-sound channel; `{7C}` EndSound was reverse-engineered static + dynamic (2026-07-10) and is implemented in Godot as a stop-all of the event SFX/BGM. The meaning of each bank slot is wiki catalog knowledge (not recoverable from the extracted `.feds`/`.json` data) and is kept hand-authored in the project (`sfx_bank_names.json` behind the runtime `SfxCatalog.gd`). The `{21}` handler landed in Godot (2026-06-28) as a fire-and-forget `SfxRouter.play_system_by_id` call, verified across the Orbonne chapel cinematic (7 cues, incl. system slot 72 'Locked Door' at PC 92). The `{60}` Fade Sound semantics were reverse-engineered end-to-end and live-verified on 2026-06-28 (pcsx-agent probes D1–D6 on `orbonne_priest_walk`): a music-only latch (`DAT_80165fc0`) that always ramps `forcePlayedMUS` to silence over `(Time << (Shift+2)) & 0x3FFC` sequencer ticks via the shared `Calc_Mus_VolChange` ramp engine — the decay steps on the music-sequencer tick (~490 Hz at scenario-1 tempo), not the video frame; godot-learning implements it as `ScenarioVM` 0x60 → `MusicPlayer.fade_out` → the smd-player sequencer master fade. The `{22}` Switch Track semantics closed statically (no dynamic rig needed) and shipped to godot-learning (2026-06-29): the track byte is only a set-gate, and the engine alternates a toggle between two pre-loaded slots holding the scenario's `music_file_one_id`/`music_file_two_id`, activating slot `toggle+1` and fading it in to a transformed Volume over `Time*4` ticks on the same `Calc_Mus_VolChange` engine.
