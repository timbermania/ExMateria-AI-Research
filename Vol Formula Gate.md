# Vol Formula Gate

The vol formula gate is bit 0x100 of `chan_word_1` (channel + 0x02, the high word of the async flag pair): when set, the per-slot IRQ handler runs the SPU volume formula and commits the channel volume; when clear, it zeroes the per-slot volume accumulator and skips. Discovered 2026-05-13 when `cure_4_no_music` fired 723 times in ROM but 350 in Godot — the gate drains faster than it rearms, costing 373 formula invocations (per-slot drain already matched 972/972, and every Godot fire that happened landed on a ROM-firing tick, so the drain side was faithful; the deficit was arming). The arming gap closed 2026-05-20 ([[Cure 4 Audio Parity]]). Arm/drain sites are shared with [[LFO Sub-Slot Period Reset]] and [[SPU Voice Engine]]; the open target-word question for the period-reset OR (does it land on `chan_word_0` or `chan_word_1`?) is CONTESTED in [[LFO Sub-Slot Period Reset]].
