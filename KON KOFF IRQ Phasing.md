# KON KOFF IRQ Phasing

FFT's RCnt2 IRQ handler (FUN_800149DC) commits effect SFX KON and KOFF in two distinct phases of the same IRQ — KON inside the per-active-entity catch-up sub-loop, KOFF in the post-loop wrap-up — and the "KOFF on cadence N, re-KEY on N+1" split seen by the probe is produced by the KON commit's deferral through the `entity+0x60` field (read at IRQ start), not by any bytecode-dispatch delay. The idle_timeout drain sets the KOFF flag and KON_ARM together under one guard, and its `byte_7A` case selector (15/16/other) drives the KOFF-tick choice. As of 2026-05-19 the Godot reimplementation is end-to-end FFT-faithful on this data flow: the two Godot-only workarounds that fought the delay (Phase A periodic re-KEY, Phase B early-KOFF lookahead) are both deleted, and the only remaining structural divergence — the delayed-rotation abstraction — is bit-exact against reraise's 148/148 probe pairs. A 2026-05-12 static reading of the commit sites pins the per-IRQ batch invariant: the kon/koff masks are accumulated over the whole slot walk and committed once per IRQ, so the decision is made on the FINAL state of the walk, not the entry state. A 2026-05-17 first-KON cross-check resolves the 4–5-row first-KEYON register-probe deficit as a probe-gate trace artifact: the SPU register state at the first KON of reraise voices 18–21 is identical on both sides, so the deficit has zero audio impact. A 2026-05-15 protect_no_music root cause (`PROTECT_2_10`) pins the post-dispatch KON-arming OR site (PC 0x80015478–0x80015490) to the inline Note state setup (`smd_note_state_setup` @ 0x80015428), reachable only for dispatched bytes < 0x80 — Rest (0x80) and control opcodes (0xA0+) route to the control dispatcher, whose handlers return to `smd_dispatcher_post_handler_check` (0x80015504) without visiting the OR site — and closes the resulting Godot over-fire (pre-fix 3 divergent `probe_fun80017118_clear` rows; post-fix 401/401 PAIR) by gating the OR on a `note_dispatched` flag. The same session's residual catalog (2026-05-15, `PROTECT_POST_EC_LIFT_RESIDUALS`) adds one remaining cosmetic residual: on the one cadence where both voices 20 and 21 commit ADSR2 writes, the intra-cadence per-voice commit order is inverted PCSX↔Godot — zero audio impact, and the validator pairs the adsr2 probes by voice within the cadence. The 2026-05-16 reraise root-cause doc (`RERAISE_VOICE_18_PERIODIC_KEYON_MISSING`) names the ROM identity of the drain block: it is FFT's periodic re-KEY mechanism — a second per-channel countdown that, under the `0x600` gate, wraps to zero to arm the next tick's primary KEYON for loop-driven repeating-note bytecodes (reraise voice 18's Δ=10 trill: 57 PCSX KEYONs vs 2 Godot in the pre-fix baseline), with Godot's interim "Phase A periodic re-KEY" workaround for that deficit being what the faithful drain superseded. A 2026-05-17 pitch-register deficit doc (`V21_PITCH_REGISTER_OVER_FIRE_DEFICIT`) pins the EndBar handler's role as the drain's between-Notes off-switch: its low-bit clear of `chan_word_0` (PC 0x800159CC, `andi 0xfffc`) closes the pitch-LFO gate that otherwise re-fires the drain path on every Note of a loop.
