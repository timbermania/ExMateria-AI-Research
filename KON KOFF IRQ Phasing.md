# KON KOFF IRQ Phasing

FFT's RCnt2 IRQ handler (FUN_800149DC) commits effect SFX KON and KOFF in two distinct phases of the same IRQ — KON inside the per-active-entity catch-up sub-loop, KOFF in the post-loop wrap-up — and the "KOFF on cadence N, re-KEY on N+1" split seen by the probe is produced by the KON commit's deferral through the `entity+0x60` field (read at IRQ start), not by any bytecode-dispatch delay. The idle_timeout drain sets the KOFF flag and KON_ARM together under one guard, and its `byte_7A` case selector (15/16/other) drives the KOFF-tick choice. As of 2026-05-19 the Godot reimplementation is end-to-end FFT-faithful on this data flow: the two Godot-only workarounds that fought the delay (Phase A periodic re-KEY, Phase B early-KOFF lookahead) are both deleted, and the only remaining structural divergence — the delayed-rotation abstraction — is bit-exact against reraise's 148/148 probe pairs. A 2026-05-12 static reading of the commit sites pins the per-IRQ batch invariant: the kon/koff masks are accumulated over the whole slot walk and committed once per IRQ, so the decision is made on the FINAL state of the walk, not the entry state.
