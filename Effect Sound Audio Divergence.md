# Effect Sound Audio Divergence

State of the Godot effect-sound synth vs the PCSX-Redux capture for `cure_no_music` as of the 2026-05-16 post-walker-flag-fix doc (`AUDIO_PARITY_NEXT_STEPS_V2`): the walker flag word entry shortfall is closed — the end-of-note ADSR2_LOW write (commit 9bf658cc) was the single biggest mover (full_mix cos_dist 0.38→0.31, voice_21 0.47→0.37; `probe_walker_flag_word_entry` pairs 320/320 within ±1 drift, 318/320 bit-exact) — and the pitch chain re-pairs with its write cadence now FFT-faithful. Residual divergences at that date: 2 init-seed walker entries (Godot seeds 0xC5 vs PCSX's 0x1FF, missing the ADSR1/SAMPLE_ADDR/VOL_LR_SWEEP arms), 2 extra PCSX walker exits via `chan_word_0 & 0x500` (`probe_slur_propagation_pre` 81/79), a 2-row `_fan_vol_lr_raw` excess at voice-21 ramp start (`probe_vol_register` 122/124, values aligned), plus still-unprobed ADSR1-register and KON/KOFF-mask paths. The 2026-05-12 accounts below are historical: the 0.45 residual was the pre-fix baseline, and the 1-cadence pitch-offset account is superseded. The 2026-05-13 CAUSE_A_PRESTAGE_TIMING doc re-attributes the "init-seed" 0x1FF entries to bind-time AC dispatch (PC `0x80017088`), and the follow-up Godot fix (deferred `walker_seed_pending` arm, synthetic first_keyon arm removed) landed since. The 2026-05-18 death (E030) V21 deficit closed a second divergence class: Godot's 1.24 s truncation of voice 21 was the missing 3rd `play_sound` — a BATTLE.BIN-driven global-bank trigger (`0x8006B97C`, sound_id 0x45) structurally outside the effect-only renderer, resolved by smd-player's catalog replay (see [[Battle Action SFX]]). The 2026-05-24 ENV_VOL_ARCHITECTURAL_GATING doc localized the cross-session env_vol divergence (the seven `probe_envelope_tail` env-tail counts) to the SPU mixer's per-sample env_vol evolution — ADSR1/ADSR2 register writes are bit-identical across all sessions, ruling out chan-side dispatch — and refuted its own stop-flag root cause (Godot already implements the ADPCM stop-sign voice-off; see [[SPU Voice Engine]]); the GATING doc was then retracted as the wrong hypothesis (commit 0f7d2bee) and the 2026-05-24 follow-up ENV_VOL_WITHIN_IRQ_KEY_ON_TIMING doc promotes within-IRQ KEY-ON sample offset to the leading candidate — PCSX commits KEY-ON at a wall-clock-dependent SPU sample inside the IRQ (probe load stretches `samples_per_cadence` to 190–320 vs canonical 183) while Godot fires it at a fixed in-sub-tick offset, so the post-KEY-ON mix-call count per cadence differs (an estimated ~230-mix-call gap plausibly matches voice-20's Δ≈7328 env); still open pending the doc's §4 KEY-ON-offset diagnostics. The 2026-05-19 flare (E031) no-music capture (post-V21-pitch-fix) adds an upstream timeline-driver class: Godot fires its two early `play_sound` calls 24 cadences early (cad 88 vs 112), binds only one pool pair per call (pair 0–1 never bound → V16/V17 silent), and never fires the third call at cad ≈ 646, leaving the layer-3 per-slot probes ~20% under-fired. The 2026-05-19 sibling flare amplitude-deficit doc (`FLARE_VOICE_18_AMPLITUDE_DEFICIT`) isolates V18 (pool slot 2) as the one flare voice whose synthesis inputs pair bit-exact yet whose render is 3.2× quieter than PCSX (mean_abs 0.198 vs 0.637, cos_dist 0.283, cos_dist_phase 0.527), localizing the defect to the SPU-mixer stage: the per-voice vol registers hold 0/0 with no explicit rewrite between KON and KOFF, PCSX alone shows an active mode-1 vol LFO on the channel's subslot 1 (depth 256) with no Godot seed/advance rows for slot 2, and the two voice-event dumpers disagree on `fmod` semantics — unresolved at the doc's date, with the subslot-1 LFO seed the leading suspect. The 2026-05-24 haste breakdown doc (post-wf_idx=4-sawtooth-fix and post-position-invariant-call_index-fix) classifies the remaining `haste_no_music` divergence: voice 21's raw_pitch (150/186 paired IRQs, cos_dist bimodal 0.03–0.66) is the real remaining audio-impactful gap; voice 20's env_vol (169 rows) is audio-irrelevant (silent FM driver); and the audible slots' `pre_pitch_hi` rows are probe-ordering offset (the lfo_handler probe emit since relocated to the BOTTOM of `cadence_body`, post-dispatch, matching PCSX BP @ `0x800174C8`) or dormant-slot music-engine residue (see [[Dormant Slot Residue]]).
