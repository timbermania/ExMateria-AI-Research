# Scenario Camera Framing

Where the scenario/event camera is aimed — the framing axis (where the screen centre falls in the world) and what happens to it across a yaw change — distinct from the speed/interpolation question (see [[Scenario Camera Opcodes]]). As of 2026-06-29 both framing axes are root-caused against the live GTE and fixed to the pixel: the PSX view is a fixed-camera-at-origin affine-ortho projection that frames the optical centre LOW (native-Y 160, not the 120 midpoint), the scenario camera sets an absolute interpolated pose with no terrain read, the horizontal scale was already correct via the project-wide `psx_par` PAR stretch, and the Godot rig needed the camera body's depth flipped the ADR-0052 way, while the floor-aim's focal-tile lookup stays un-flipped (it indexes `map_composer.get_tile` in Godot-world coordinates). `camera_position_offset` is back to ZERO — the fixes are authentic, not fudges; a constant world-space offset was proven pivot-breaking across rotation (its projected screen shift `(−δ·right, −δ·up)` rotates with the camera, so one tuned value is wrong at every other pose) and removed outright (ADR-0057).
